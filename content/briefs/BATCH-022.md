**Chapter feel.** The nine `w5_*` trails are small poems: a mood in three or four steps at level 3. Use a time or a
weather condition, a place, and an ordered action ("before / after"). Keep a title when its theme is reachable;
otherwise retitle. The three watchtower trails are about guarding the place you have built:
- `frostwacht`: a tower in the snow;
- `gartenwacht`: a garden guard;
- `vogelpfad`: birds, parrots and chickens, a lookout.
