# BATCH-021 plan: wonders of the world, far lands and great feasts (level 3)

Brief: `content/briefs/BATCH-021.md` on top of BATCH-016 to BATCH-019 and `L3-PLAN.md`. Thirteen trails; ids, `order`, `requires` and step ids (s1, s2, s3, finale) are kept.
Every trail is level 3 by tree depth (7 to 9), so none is rebuilt with -아/어야 해요. Every clue is generated; only the finale lines are authored (ten of them; three use the generated ceremony line).
Nothing here needs a structure: temples, shipwrecks and treasure are reduced to what a goal can check.
