# BATCH-020 plan: explorers, far views and high places (level 3)

Brief: `content/briefs/BATCH-020.md` on top of BATCH-016 to BATCH-018 and `L3-PLAN.md`. Fifteen trails; ids, `order`, `requires` and the old step ids (s1, s2, s3, finale) are kept; trails that had only two steps gain an s3, and polarlicht and eiskrone an s4.
Every trail is level 3 by tree depth (depth 7 to 9), so no trail needs the -아/어야 해요 ending.
Every clue is generated; only titles and finale lines are authored. The grammar list is in each trail file and in `manifest.json`.
The vanilla titles are not kept: none of the trails can check a biome tour, a sherd, a template or an elytra flight, so none earns its advancement. The End trails (the dragon, the End biomes) are rebuilt in the overworld and proposed for a higher level.
