# BATCH-018 plan: heroes of the village (story, guilds and trade; level 3)

Brief: `content/briefs/BATCH-018.md` on top of BATCH-016/017 and `L3-PLAN.md`. Fifteen trails; ids, `order`, `requires` and, where the old trail had them,
step ids (s1.., finale) are kept. All fifteen sit at level 3 by tree depth (7 to 9), so none is rebuilt with -아/어야 해요.
Every clue is generated; only the finale lines (and the new titles) are authored. The old trails were generated filler (cinnabar bricks, pottery sherds),
so each one is a new small project. The grammar list is in each trail file and in `manifest.json`.

Only `adv_adventure_whos_the_pillager_now` keeps its vanilla title: its goal does check a pillager defeated with a crossbow in hand. The other nine advancement
trails are retitled because they no longer award the advancement (the guild trails and the demo have no vanilla title); `manifest.json` lists six of them as
proposals for a higher level.
