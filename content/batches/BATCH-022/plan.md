# BATCH-022 plan: small poems I and the watchtowers (level 3)

Brief: `content/briefs/BATCH-022.md` on top of BATCH-016 to BATCH-018 and `L3-PLAN.md`. Twelve trails; ids, `order`, `requires`, `tier` and step ids (s1, s2, s3, finale) are kept. `gartenwacht` had two steps and gains an `s3`.
Every trail is level 3 by tree depth (7 or 8), so none is rebuilt with -아/어야 해요. Every clue is generated; only the finale lines are authored (all twelve).
The old trails were random item lists (lime harness, dead brain coral wall fan, magma cube). Each one is now a small poem: a place or time, an ordered action, a "don't", and a finale that claims only what its goal checks.
