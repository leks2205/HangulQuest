## Applied

- **`weather_snow`:**
  - Snowballs cannot be crafted in Java. The line now only says you held one: 눈덩이를 들었어요.
  - 어제 밤 is written as one word: 어젯밤.
- **`field_harvest`:**
  - No real-world watering. The hoe turns the soil into a field (흙을 밭으로 만들어요).
  - The milk now comes from the cow (소에게서 우유를 얻어요), instead of the cow giving it after being fed.
- **Verb collocations:**
  - `colors_dye`: 양털을 깎았어요.
  - `meals_cake`: 우유를 얻었어요.
  - `nether_fortress`: 물을 담았어요.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - `festival_night`: 어젯밤.
  - `animals_wolf`: the past tense is kept.
  - `field_flowers`: the sunflower sentence now names its object.
  - `animals_bees`: 벌집에 갔어요.
- **Dialogue:**
  - `farm`: 밭에 심어요.
  - `maps`: the gloss now says "under the sea".
  - `cleric`: the trade line reads 금 세 개에 에메랄드 한 개예요; 에 marks an exchange rate.
- **Kept:**
  - The `nether_fortress` brewing paragraph skips the blaze-powder fuel. It is an omission, not a false claim.
  - Both reviewers preferred 바닷속 and 숲속. These compounds are not yet in the learner lexicon, so the line uses the
    known words 바다 아래에 and 숲에.
