Help review the Korean

Hangul Quest is a Minecraft mod that teaches Korean. Every Korean line below is checked by a linter and two AI reviewers, but only native speakers can say that a line really sounds natural. Read a line, press Looks right or Suggest a correction. A GitHub account is needed; approvals count from accounts at least 30 days old, and nobody can approve their own line.
