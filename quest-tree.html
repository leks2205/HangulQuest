Reading the tree

  • Roots have no requires.completed. There are several so a new player can start in the branch that matches what they are doing anyway.
  • Merges (a trail with two or more parents) are the interesting nodes: they only unlock when every parent is finished, which is what turns a list of questlines into a tree.
  • Hints never leave level 1. A late trail speaks literary Korean; pressing H restates the same task in polite Korean, so the ladder never blocks you.
  • The ritual finale is the guarantee. Every trail ends with an action you can always perform — a torch at night, a campfire on a beach — never a structure or a coordinate.
