# Review, streak and achievements

A word you met once is not a word you know. Hangul Quest keeps a Leitner-style schedule for every word
and quietly turns the words you are about to forget back into quests.

## The schedule

Seven buckets, from "just met" (0) to "mastered" (6):

| Level | Comes back after |
|---|---|
| 0 | 10 minutes |
| 1 | 5 minutes |
| 2 | 30 minutes |
| 3 | 2 hours |
| 4 | 8 hours |
| 5 | 1 day |
| 6 | 3 days |

- A **correct** answer moves a word one level up, a **wrong** answer one level down. Six correct
  answers in a row take a word from fresh to mastered.
- A **practice** answer — the hint sidequest, an ambient word card — moves a word *up* when correct
  but never down. Practice is exposure, not an exam.
- A word answered wrong comes back after two minutes, inside the same session, while you still
  remember what tripped you.

## What counts as a review

Three things feed the schedule:

1. **Flashcards** in the journal's **Lernen** tab, and the `J` key.
2. **Quest steps**: finishing a step that teaches a word counts as a successful review of it.
3. **The adaptive trail** (below), which is the same review in quest form.

## The adaptive review trail

Once a day the mod builds a trail called **복습의 길**, *the path of review*, out of the words that are
due and that map to something you can do in the world. It is seeded by world name and date, so it is
the same trail all day and changes tomorrow. It sits outside the main tree and is always available —
you never lose your place in the story by reviewing.

## Streak

The streak counts days on which you did at least one review or finished a step, in real-world dates.
Missing a day resets the current streak but keeps your best one. The **Info** tab shows both.

## Achievements

Fifteen, all unlocked quietly:

| Group | Achievements |
|---|---|
| Vocabulary | 1, 10, 25, 50 and 100 words discovered |
| Mastery | 10 words mastered |
| Reviews | 100 reviews |
| Streak | 3, 7 and 30 days in a row |
| Categories | 10 animals, 25 blocks |
| Quests | first trail, three trails, five sidequests |

Each has a Korean name, a German name and a vanilla icon. The **Erfolge** tab lists them with progress;
unlocked ones are stamped with the date.

## Why the mod is quiet about numbers

There is no XP bar for Korean, no daily goal you can fail and no notification asking you to come back.
The counters exist because they help you see progress, not because the mod wants a habit loop. If you
prefer it even quieter, switch off **Auto-Quiz** and the HUD cards in the journal's options and use only
`K` and `H`.
