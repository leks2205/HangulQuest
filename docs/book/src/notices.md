## Vanilla names

`data/names.json` is generated by `tools/extract_names.py` from the Korean and German language files of
the installed game. It contains item, block, entity and biome **names** only — no models, textures, sounds
or other assets. Quest text written on top of those names is original.

## Advancements

Some questlines are themed around vanilla advancements; only their names and completion conditions are
referenced, and no advancement data is redistributed.

## Lute

The vocabulary import parses [Lute](https://github.com/LuteOrg/lute-v3)'s CSV export (MIT). Lute is not
bundled and is never written to.

## Build tools

Gradle (Apache-2.0), Fabric Loom (Apache-2.0) and JUnit 5 (EPL-2.0) build and test the mod; they are not
distributed with it.
